How I designed it

A rate is not a finding.

The output had to move from a broad funnel observation to a specific, reviewable action. Each specialist investigated one breakpoint. The synthesis layer ranked only findings that could survive these six questions.

PopulationWho or what is affected?
Broken flowWhich rule, route, or step needs inspection?
DurabilityIs it persistent or a one-period fluctuation?
EvidenceWhat source and validation support it?
OwnershipWho investigates, approves, and closes it?
ConfidenceWhat can we say, and what remains unproven?
